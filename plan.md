# Training plan (US Eastern)

## Day-trading practice - Monday to Friday, market days only
- 8:45-9:25am: premarket prep. Watchlist, overnight news, key levels.
- 9:30-11:00am: paper trading only. No real money until the plan says so.
- 4:15-4:45pm: review. Journal every trade: setup, entry, exit, what went right or wrong.

## Fitness
- Monday, Wednesday, Friday 6:00-7:00pm: full-body strength.
- Tuesday, Thursday 6:00-6:45pm: cardio / conditioning.
- Saturday 10:00-11:00am: long session or sport.
- Sunday: rest, plus 20 minutes of mobility.
